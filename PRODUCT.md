# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

KCIC's website serves climate innovators and programme applicants, development partners, donors, investors, government and research partners, programme participants, job candidates, suppliers, media, and the public. Visitors need to understand KCIC quickly, find relevant programmes and evidence, and reach the right opportunity or contact path without institutional friction.

## Product Purpose

The website presents Kenya Climate Innovation Center as a credible African catalyst for climate entrepreneurship. It explains KCIC's mission, work, programmes, and measurable impact; connects entrepreneurs with support; and gives partners and the public clear access to institutional information, evidence, opportunities, and current activity. Success means visitors can recognize KCIC's role within seconds and confidently take the next relevant action.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, enterprising, and hopeful. KCIC should feel close to real Kenyan innovators and communities, rigorous enough for institutional partners, and optimistic because its work turns climate challenges into practical enterprise opportunities.

## Anti-references

The site should not resemble a generic green NGO template filled with leaf motifs and anonymous stock landscapes. It should not look like a presentation deck transferred directly to the web, a busy corporate portal, or a glossy technology startup that makes claims without evidence. Avoid automatic hero carousels, excessive glass effects, decorative sustainability clichés, and data displays without dates or definitions.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with climate entrepreneurs and real solutions, not abstract environmental symbolism.
2. Make the mission understandable before asking the visitor to explore the organization.
3. Pair optimism with evidence, using dated and clearly defined impact claims.
4. Give distinct audiences direct, visible routes to programmes, impact, opportunities, and contact.
5. Use KCIC's circles, arcs, green, cyan, and documentary photography with restraint and purpose.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA. The website must support keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, high-contrast text and controls, semantic structure, useful image alternatives, 320-pixel reflow, and 200% zoom. It must not rely on color, imagery, hover, or motion alone to communicate meaning. Photography and language should present entrepreneurs and communities with agency and dignity.
