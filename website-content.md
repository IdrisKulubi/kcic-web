## 4. About KCIC

### 4.1 Who We Are — web draft

KCIC is a non-profit organization that supports the development and scale-up of locally relevant climate technologies and enterprises. It was established in 2012 as the first center in a global Climate Innovation Center network initiated by the World Bank's infoDev programme. After initially operating through a consortium, KCIC became independent in 2015.

KCIC was founded on the belief that climate challenges can become opportunities for innovation, enterprise, jobs, and resilient communities. Its work brings together entrepreneurship support, climate finance, technical assistance, market knowledge, partnerships, and policy engagement.

### 4.4 Strategy 4.0 — web draft

Heading: **From individual ventures to thriving ecosystems.**

KCIC's 2025-2030 strategy is designed to strengthen climate entrepreneurship and resilience at ecosystem level. It builds on more than a decade of enterprise support and positions KCIC as a climate ecosystem catalyst in Africa.

The strategy responds to four persistent barriers:

- limited access to finance and technical assistance;
- inadequate market systems and enabling environments;
- insufficient community engagement and resilience; and
- the need for stronger institutional capacity.

The presentation refers to an Integrated Climate Entrepreneurship Ecosystem (ICEE) model. Before building the Theory of Change page, obtain the approved diagram and definitions for every component and relationship.

## 6. Key sectors

### 6.1 Renewable Energy

KCIC supports solutions in off-grid solar and mini-grids, productive uses of energy, clean cooking, bioenergy and waste-to-energy, energy efficiency and demand-side management, and green financing.

### 6.2 Circular Economy

KCIC supports solid-waste management and recycling, green manufacturing and product-life extension, upcycling and eco-design, plastic alternatives and biodegradable materials, industrial symbiosis, and resource recovery.

### 6.3 Mobility

KCIC supports electric mobility, sustainable public transport, mobility-as-a-service and digital platforms, non-motorized transport, logistics and freight optimization, and battery charging or swapping.

### 6.4 Nature-Based Solutions

KCIC supports ecosystem restoration and afforestation, forestry and agroforestry, blue-economy and ecosystem-restoration initiatives, biodiversity and ecotourism, carbon-credit solutions, and community natural-resource management.

### 6.5 Water

KCIC supports water harvesting and storage, wastewater reuse, water, sanitation and hygiene solutions, smart irrigation, water monitoring and purification, and integrated water management.

### 6.6 Agriculture

KCIC supports climate-smart agriculture, agribusiness and value addition, irrigation and water efficiency, post-harvest and storage solutions, agri-tech including precision tools and drones, and market-access and financing solutions.

Editorial note: confirm the preferred capitalization of “Nature-Based Solutions,” whether “Water” includes WASH in the public taxonomy, and whether green finance belongs inside Renewable Energy or should be cross-cutting.
